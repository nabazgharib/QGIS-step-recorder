description=Records your clicks, menu choices, dialog settings and map actions and turns them into step-by-step tutorials with screenshots.
about=Step Recorder watches what you do in QGIS (menus, toolbar buttons, dialog fields, Processing algorithms, map canvas clicks, layer changes) and writes it down as numbered, human-readable instructions - like the "Steps Recorder" tool in Windows. Every step can get an annotated screenshot. Edit, re-order or delete steps, add your own notes, then export the tutorial to Markdown, HTML, PDF, plain text or JSON. Instruction language: English, Arabic (العربية) or Kurdish Sorani (کوردی سۆرانی).
